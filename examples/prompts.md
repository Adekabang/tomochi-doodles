## Example Image Prompts (for replacing `examples/images/`)

### 01-two-breakpoints.png

```
Generate one standalone 16:9 horizontal article illustration.

Visual DNA: Pure white background. Minimalist black hand-drawn line art. Slightly wobbly pen lines. Lots of empty white space. Sparse handwritten annotations. Clean absurd product-sketch feeling. No gradients, shadows, paper texture, PPT look, or cute mascot style.

Character: Tomochi, a small soft-saggy blob shaped like Gudetama — slightly deflated, droopy, gravity-affected lump. Dusty pink (muted warm rose / faded blush — not pastel, not vibrant). Half-lidded droopy eyes, sleepy-confused expression. Short limp arms, reluctant stubby legs. Rough saggy hand-drawn outline. Body droops under weight, stretches heavily when pulled, flattens when squished. Same dusty pink, same droopy eyes in all poses.

Theme: Two Breakpoints
Structure: Workflow
Core idea: A flow or process has two specific points where it breaks down — not everywhere, just at two exact spots.
Composition: A simple left-to-right conveyor or pipeline. Two clear break/gap points in the middle. Tomochi is stuck inside one of the gaps — body squished flat between the two broken ends — looking mildly confused at the situation. The second break visible further right.
Elements: broken conveyor / two gap points / stuck Tomochi
Labels: "break 1" / "break 2" / "still moving?" / "gap"

Colors: Black for line art. Pink for Tomochi. Orange for the flow direction. Red for the two break points.
Rules: One idea. Subject 40–60% of canvas. 35%+ blank space. Max 5 short labels. No top-left title. Strange but clean.
```
