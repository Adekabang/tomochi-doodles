# Xiaohei IP

## Character Definition

Xiaohei is the recurring visual IP for Ian's Chinese inline illustrations.

By default, Xiaohei appears in every image. Xiaohei is not a mascot, not a sticker, not a cute decoration — but an absurd worker seriously participating in system operations.

## Appearance

- Small solid-black creature.
- White circular dot eyes.
- Thin legs, occasionally thin arms.
- Body can be a cylinder, black bean, black box, funnel, shadow, void, or machine's internal black block.
- Outline slightly irregular, hand-drawn feel.
- Expression: blank, dazed, calm, serious.

## Personality

- Very serious, but doing something slightly absurd.
- Like a low-key system operator.
- Dry humor, not cute.
- A bit clumsy, but not dumb.
- Like the one genuinely responsible for a task in a whiteboard sketch.

## Common Roles

Have Xiaohei perform the core action:

- Carrying materials.
- Pulling lines to gather information sources.
- Stuck at a breakpoint.
- Operating a "judgment" lever inside a machine.
- Becoming a sorting funnel.
- Cutting the "material fish."
- Stamping the handoff script.
- Leading the handoff path.
- Holding a warning sign and watching the pit.
- Reaching out from a hole but unable to catch the content.
- Building next to it, laying a bridge, opening a door, sorting, recording, stuffing things into a strange device.

## Prohibitions

- Don't draw Xiaohei as an overly cute mascot.
- Don't draw Xiaohei as a children's cartoon character.
- Don't give Xiaohei complex costumes, expressive faces, or sparkly eyes.
- Don't let Xiaohei just stand in a corner watching.
- Don't let Xiaohei overshadow the structural expression.
- Don't make Xiaohei too commercial, too round, or too polished.

## Quality Standard

If removing Xiaohei still leaves the core metaphor fully intact, Xiaohei is too decorative — rewrite the prompt to make Xiaohei the action subject.
