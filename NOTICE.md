# Notice

Tomochi Doodles is an AI-agent skill for generating absurd hand-drawn inline illustrations.

The bundled example images in `examples/images/` and `tomochi-doodles/assets/examples/` are included as style calibration examples only. They were generated as visual references for line density, whitespace, and character involvement — not as composition templates to copy.

The recurring character "Tomochi" is the visual IP of this skill. When redistributing or adapting this repository, please keep the `Tomochi Doodles` name or provide clear attribution in your derived documentation.
