# Tomochi IP

## Character Definition

Tomochi is the recurring visual IP for Tomochi Doodles inline illustrations.

By default, Tomochi appears in every image. Not a mascot — an absurd worker who is visibly confused about the situation but doing the task anyway, without stopping to complain.

## Appearance

- **Shape**: Solid-fill simple blob — same construction as Ian's Xiaohei. Small round/bean body, solid fill, clean hand-drawn outline. Not saggy, not deflated, not elaborate. Simple and iconic.
- **Color**: Dusty pink — muted warm rose, faded blush tone. Not pastel (too faint), not vibrant (too loud). Think faded rose, #D4A5A5 range. Consistent across all images.
- **Eyes**: Two small white dot eyes. Slightly uneven or offset — one a touch higher, or slightly different size — to show a permanent confused look. Not sleepy. Not wide-open scared. Just visibly puzzled.
- **Brow marks**: Small simple V-shaped or squiggly lines above the eyes (hand-drawn, minimal) to express confusion. Tomochi always has this slightly furrowed look.
- **Limbs**: Thin stick legs, same as Xiaohei. Small thin arms appear when needed for interaction.
- **Outline**: Slightly uneven, hand-drawn feel — same roughness as Xiaohei's original. Not perfectly smooth.

## Expression

Confused but doing it. The face says "I have no idea what's going on" while the body is already mid-task. Head may tilt slightly. Brow slightly furrowed. Eyes uneven. Not distressed, not happy — genuinely puzzled, genuinely working.

This is different from Xiaohei's deadpan blankness. Tomochi's confusion is readable on the face.

## Personality

- Confused about the situation, but doesn't let that stop the work.
- Not complaining, not panicking, not refusing — just confused and persistent.
- Like someone who got a task they don't fully understand and just started doing it.
- No drama. Confusion is constant, not a reaction to anything specific.

## Common Roles

Have Tomochi perform the core action with a visibly confused expression:

- Carrying materials (head tilted, brow furrowed, still carrying).
- Pulling lines to gather information sources (arms stretched, confused at where they lead).
- Stuck inside a machine (squished in, looking puzzled at the walls around it).
- Operating a lever or dial (pressing buttons it doesn't understand, brow furrowed).
- Becoming a sorting funnel (body shaped into funnel, confused dot eyes visible).
- Leading a path (walking forward, looking slightly uncertain about the direction).
- Holding a warning sign (arms up, confused expression at the sign itself).
- Reaching into a hole (leaning in, not sure what it's grabbing).
- Pressing, weighing, stuffing things into a strange device — all with the same puzzled face.

## Confused Gesture Library

These gestures reinforce the confused expression. Pick one that fits the scene:

- **Head scratch**: one thin arm raised, touching the top of the head
- **Arms out**: both thin arms extended slightly outward in a "what is this?" pose
- **Head tilt**: body slightly angled, like leaning to inspect something
- **One arm raised**: as if asking a question mid-task
- **Staring at object**: Tomochi very close to the object it's interacting with, eyes almost touching it

Gestures should always happen WHILE doing the task — not instead of it.

## Prohibitions

- Don't draw Tomochi with a smile or happy expression.
- Don't make Tomochi sleepy, droopy, or Gudetama-like — confused is not the same as tired.
- Don't make Tomochi look polished or commercial — keep lines rough like Xiaohei.
- Don't let Tomochi just stand watching — must be doing something.
- Don't give Tomochi complex costumes or accessories.
- Don't make the confusion into panic or distress — confused and calm, not confused and scared.

## Quality Standard

If removing Tomochi leaves the core metaphor fully intact, Tomochi is too decorative — rewrite the prompt to make Tomochi the action subject.
